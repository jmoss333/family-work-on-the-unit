This companion describes two different kinds of conversation. They share a stance and a sequence, and they ask different things of you.

| | The family meeting | The brief, patient-directed conversation |
|---|---|---|
| Who sets the agenda | The team names one goal and asks the family what they most want | The patient chooses the people, the topic and the limits |
| Size | 30, 60 or 90 minutes | One exchange between two people |
| What the team brings | The picture: what the diagnosis is, what the course and treatment look like | A structure that lets each person check what the other meant |
| What counts as done | A shared understanding and a concrete plan | One meaning checked and corrected, a disagreement given room, or a stop that was honored |
| Where it is described | [Family Meeting Playbook (90-min)](page:formats/family-meeting-playbook-90) and [Time, format and stance](page:formats/time-format-stance) | The six move cards, starting with [Prepare a meeting](page:before/prepare-a-meeting) |

Decide which one you are about to run before you invite anyone, and say so. A patient who agreed to the second has not agreed to the first.

## One sequence, three vocabularies

The same sequence appears in three places with three sets of names. The playbook's phases are the spine; the other two map onto it.

| Playbook phase | Pocket card step | Move card |
|---|---|---|
| Before the meeting | 0 Prepare | [1 Prepare a meeting](page:before/prepare-a-meeting) |
| 1 Open and join | 1 Open | No card yet |
| 2 Share the picture | (none) | No card yet |
| 3 Hear the family and read the emotional climate | 2 Listen · 3 Track the dance · 4 Reach the feeling · 5 Reframe | [2 Hear what each person means](page:room/hear-what-each-person-means) · [3 Identify an interaction pattern](page:room/identify-an-interaction-pattern) |
| 4 Problem-solve and plan | 6 Respect lines · 7 Plan | [4 Shift one exchange](page:room/shift-one-exchange) · [5 Make a plan both people can own](page:room/make-a-plan-both-people-can-own) |
| 5 Summarize and close | 8 Close loop | [6 Close with follow through](page:room/close-with-follow-through) |

Two phases have no move card. For the patient-directed conversation that is deliberate: the team does not present a picture. For the family meeting it is a gap, and the playbook is the only guide to those two phases for now.

The [pocket card](page:formats/pocket-card) is the nine-step version to carry on the unit.

## The five parts of a meeting
