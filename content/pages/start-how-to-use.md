This is a working companion for residents and attending physicians who run family and support-person conversations on adult inpatient psychiatry units. It is built for three kinds of use.

**A three-minute lookup.** Before a meeting, a supervision hour or rounds: find the move you need, read one short card, borrow a question.

**A ten-minute rehearsal.** With a supervisor or a colleague: say your next line aloud, see one plausible response, compare alternatives and try the same moment again.

**A workflow for the unit.** For whoever runs the unit: eight steps, each with an owner, that put the family meeting on the calendar.

## What this is not

- **Not a course of family therapy.** Each card teaches one facilitated conversation move. A course of family-focused therapy, an Open Dialogue service model, or specialist couples or family therapy requires its own training, setting and fidelity.
- **Not a protocol or legal advice.** It does not replace supervision, your unit's procedures, or your institution's privacy and legal guidance.
- **Not a place for patient information.** Every person and exchange here is invented. Nothing you do on these pages is recorded, saved or sent.

## How pages are marked

Every teaching page states its review status under the title: attested by faculty for this site, attested upstream in the Psychiatry Clerkship Library, or in review. An attestation is bound to the exact text it covers. If the text changes, the page drops back to in review until it is attested again. The [governance page](page:about/governance) shows the full ledger.
