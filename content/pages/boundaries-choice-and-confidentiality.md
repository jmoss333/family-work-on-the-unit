Involvement is negotiable. Teach the clinician to ask separately who may join, what may be discussed, how contact can happen safely, and whether anyone wants to stop.

## G1 Distinguish attendance, receiving information and disclosure

For the fictional adult with decision-making capacity, ask explicitly before inviting anyone or discussing a topic. Under HIPAA, agreement, an opportunity to object or professional judgment in specified circumstances can permit relevant sharing. Limit what is shared to information directly relevant to the person’s involvement. Staff may hear concerns without confirming someone’s admission or returning private information. A support person’s presence does not authorize a recap to other people.

[HHS guidance on sharing](https://www.hhs.gov/hipaa/for-professionals/faq/health-care-provider-permitted-discuss-adult-patients-mental-health-information-patients.html) · [HHS guidance on listening](https://www.hhs.gov/hipaa/for-professionals/faq/what-options-do-family-members-adult-patient-mental-illness-have-if-they-are-concerned-about.html)

## G2 Know when private contact takes priority

Maine’s 34-B §1207(5-A) specifies conditions for certain disclosures by licensed mental health professionals. Capacity, emergency, and serious-and-imminent-threat provisions need individual assessment. Route ambiguity, guardianship, urgent risk or applicable Part 2 records to the supervisor and current local process. Offer an appropriately supported later conversation for fatigue, distress or communication barriers. Fear of retaliation or coercion changes the plan: preserve a private route, assess safety and avoid exposing the person by explaining their disclosure in front of the other participant.

[Maine statute](https://legislature.maine.gov/statutes/34-b/title34-Bsec1207.html) · [HHS on Part 2](https://www.hhs.gov/hipaa/part-2/index.html) · [VA private screening guidance, §6(b)](https://www.va.gov/VHAPUBLICATIONS/ViewPublication.asp?pub_ID=8390)

## G3 Name the treatment accurately

NICE CG178 describes family intervention for psychosis or schizophrenia as a course of at least ten planned sessions over three months to one year with competent, supervised practitioners. It can begin during admission. Teach each card as one facilitated conversation move. A course of family-focused therapy, Open Dialogue service model, or specialist couples/family therapy requires its own training, setting and fidelity.

[NICE CG178 §§1.3.7, 1.3.9 and 1.4.4](https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/cg178/chapter/Recommendations)

## Participation by both people

Explain who can hear, that anyone can step out, and the limits of what the team or another participant can keep private. Ask each person what they are willing to discuss. Do not promise secrecy about an urgent safety issue.
