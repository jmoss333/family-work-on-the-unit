One resident, one supervisor, about ten minutes. Nurses, social workers, attendings and students can use the same cards to prepare or observe.

## What to listen for

Does the resident invite the listener to say what they heard, then give the speaker room to correct it?

## While you watch

- Quote the resident’s actual line.
- Who got to check or correct the meaning?
- Ask the same resident to try that moment again in their own words.
- If participation stops, observe how the resident responds.

## Debrief

What did the resident say to invite Sal’s reflection? Where did Rowan get a turn to correct it? Give feedback on one line and repeat the same moment. If someone declines, describe how the resident honored that choice.

## How the rehearsal runs

The supervisor listens to the first attempt, gives one specific observation and invites the same resident to try again.

The resident asks the listener to put the speaker’s meaning into their own words, then gives the speaker a turn to confirm or correct it. Either person can decline. In the private safety branch, honoring a stop completes the exercise.

## What counts

A learner can finish by making room for disagreement, honoring a stop, or arranging separate contact. Agreement with the team earns no credit.

This card is written for a fictional rehearsal. If you adapt it to observe a real conversation, record no patient or family identifiers on it.
