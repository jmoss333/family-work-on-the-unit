The [unit workflow](page:system/unit-workflow) was built for the constraints of an adult inpatient stay, not around them. Nobody on it added hours: each step removes a handoff or a second phone call. This page is for whoever wants to copy it on another unit.

## Six constraints, six answers

| The constraint | What the workflow does about it |
|---|---|
| Days, not weeks | Ask on day 1. The collateral call is the invitation. A thirty-minute meeting counts. |
| The first 72 hours are acute | Start with the 5-minute phone update. Step up to 30 minutes when sleep and arousal allow; 60–90 only when attention can hold an agenda. No major decisions in the first 72 hours. |
| Consent and legal status | Listening is not disclosing. Private check first. Scope documented, revisited, and visible to the whole team. |
| Census, discharges, two p.m. | Planned in rounds, booked by the secretary, family brought up by techs. Never improvised. |
| The note | The drafting tools give the minutes back, and every line is reviewed and signed before it reaches the chart or the family. The minutes go back to the meeting. |
| Families who can’t come in | The phone update and a scheduled call with the 30-minute structure are real formats. A short planned call with owners beats a missed meeting. |

Reclaimed administrative time becomes family time.

## Settle these locally first

The workflow names owners and records. It leaves these to your unit.

- **The screening instrument.** Use your unit’s approved brief instrument as a structured, chartable prompt for the private safety check. The private conversation is the active ingredient, not the questionnaire.
- **Three legal readings.** Confirmation from institutional counsel on guardianship, involuntary-status disclosure, and the telephone rules under 42 CFR Part 2 [G2].
- **Interpreters.** On the unit this workflow comes from, professional interpretation needs at least 24 hours of lead time. Find out your own figure. Never use a family member for a consequential conversation.
- **Hours.** Families work day jobs. Offer evening and phone options or you will systematically select for the unemployed.
- **Staffing.** One social worker may be carrying the whole unit, and meetings compete with rounds and groups. Say so out loud in planning.
- **The record.** A narrative note in the places the workflow names. No new fields in the health record.

Where the workflow and your unit’s policy differ, policy wins.

When those are settled, [Adapt it for your unit](page:system/adapt-it) rewrites the workflow with your own owners and intervals and prints it.

## Measure before you target

The workflow proposes four monthly measures, read from the chart and the calendar. Set the baseline for one month before setting targets.

## What this does not claim

No study in the [evidence register](page:evidence/register) shows that one inpatient meeting shortens a stay or prevents readmission. Family involvement during an admission has been associated with attending follow-up in the week after discharge [D3]; that is an association, not a cause. The family work with trial evidence behind it is a course that continues after the acute phase [R1, G3].

The right claim is not “this works.” It is “this is specific enough to test.”
