Invite each person to say what they understood, what is still unsettled and who may check back by what route. Ask separately about permission to share any summary.

> “What do you want the other person to remember? Who will check back, and when? What should stay between the people in this room?”

## When to use or pause

Useful before ending even if there is no shared plan. Keep a fatigued participant’s close brief. If contact is unsafe, choose a safe private follow-up or defer according to preference and unit procedure.

## Misstep and repair

“So we’re all agreed” can silence dissent. Try “I closed too quickly. What part is still yours to decide? What can I accurately say is settled?”

## Evidence and supervision

Follow-up associations concern inpatient involvement broadly \[D3\]. Teach-back and checking ownership here are expert-informed. Faculty verifies any team task, timeframe and permitted recipient before treating a promise as real.
