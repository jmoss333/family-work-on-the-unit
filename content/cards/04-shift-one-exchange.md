**First observed skill.** With both people willing, invite the listener to say what they heard. Return to the speaker so they can confirm or correct the meaning.

> “Sal, what did you hear Rowan asking for?” Then pause. “Rowan, did Sal get that, or is there something you’d change?”

## When to use or pause

Useful when both can stay engaged and keep speaking through the clinician. Avoid pressing for eye contact, an apology, trauma details or direct speech. Offer clinician-mediated or separate discussion if preferred.

## Misstep and repair

“Tell Sal that you forgive them” supplies both the words and the outcome. Try “I wrote an ending for you. What do you want Sal to understand, if anything?”

## Evidence and supervision

Direct inpatient evidence concerns longer packages \[D1, D2\]; enactment process research is extrapolated \[X1\]. Faculty should model stopping, pace one turn at a time and supervise use when conflict is escalating.
