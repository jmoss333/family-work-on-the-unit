Name two observable actions in sequence, tentatively. Let either person reject your account.

## When to use or pause

Useful after both have described a repeating exchange. If someone fears consequences for disagreeing, stop the joint exercise and check privately. Never use a “cycle” to distribute responsibility for abuse.

## Misstep and repair

“You withdraw and make Sal take over” assigns blame. Try “I made it sound like your fault. I saw a pause and heard Sal speak. What did the pause mean to you?”
